ABOUT US

Welcome to The Forward Foundation

The Forward Foundation was founded by a group of like-minded individuals aiming for holistic transformation through active citizen engagement. Leading this movement was the late Sri. Ramesh Sivaram, a key figure in water and lake rejuvenation efforts in Bengaluru.

An environmental engineer by profession, Ramesh ji had a brilliant mind and was pivotal in establishing neighborhood citizen groups throughout Bengaluru, encouraging local responsibility for water bodies and civic projects. Ramesh ji passed away on January 26, 2020, but his work and legacy continue to inspire our initiatives.

Amilineni Satheesh

Trustee — Business

Satheesh Amilineni is a seasoned business professional and social innovator with over two decades in technology and enterprise solutions. He studied Mechanical Engineering at AIT, Chikmagalur, holds an M.S. from BITS-Pilani, and an EGMP from IIM-Bangalore. He spent 16.5 years at Oracle India Pvt Ltd before founding SMARTRONIXS, where he implements IoT and software solutions that bridge technology and community impact.

At TFF, Satheesh focuses on strategic business development, building systems that make conservation scalable, measurable, and impactful. His vision: ensuring every child in rural India has clean water, healthy forests, and a liveable future — powered by communities that own and drive change.

Indrajeet Ghorpade

Advisory Board

Hailing from the illustrious Ghorpade family, a Maratha warrior clan settled in the Deccan for over 700 years, his deep and passionate love for nature and wildlife began at the Gajendragad branch, nurtured under the guidance of respected gurus.

Witnessing the drastic decline of wildlife, he founded the Deccan Conservation Foundation in 2016 to conserve, preserve and protect the last wild spaces of a human-dominated and fragmented Deccan.
